Do faster courts promote breakthrough innovation? We study China’s 2020 complex-simple civil case diversion reform, which accelerated civil dispute resolution in 20 pilot cities. Using a difference-in-differences design, we find that the reform increased breakthrough patenting by 24.9 percent but had no significant effect on total or incremental patents. Mechanism evidence points to a financial-enforcement channel: treated courts resolve debt disputes more quickly, firms obtain more trade credit, face lower financing constraints, and increase R&D investment. The effect is stronger in technologically specialized regions and litigation-intensive industries. The results suggest that judicial efficiency promotes breakthrough innovation by strengthening contract enforcement and relaxing financial frictions, even without changes in intellectual property protection.
